Our Story

Passion for tea, dedication to quality

From Garden to Cup

Founded in 2015, Serene Tea House began with a simple mission: to bring the world's finest teas to those who appreciate quality and tradition. We work directly with small-scale farmers and family-owned estates across Asia, ensuring every leaf meets our exacting standards.

Our master tea sommelier personally visits each garden, selecting only the most exceptional harvests. We believe that great tea is more than just a beverage—it's a moment of peace in a busy world, a connection to ancient traditions, and a celebration of nature's finest gifts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our teas

How should I store my tea?

Store tea in an airtight container away from light, moisture, and strong odors. Most teas stay fresh for 6-12 months when stored properly.

What's the best water temperature for brewing?

Black teas: 200-212°F (93-100°C), Green teas: 160-180°F (71-82°C), White teas: 175-185°F (79-85°C), Herbal teas: 200-212°F (93-100°C).

Do you offer caffeine-free options?

Yes! We have a wide selection of herbal teas and rooibos that are naturally caffeine-free, perfect for evening enjoyment.

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days within the US. Express shipping options are available at checkout for faster delivery.
